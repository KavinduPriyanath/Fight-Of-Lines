# Fight of Lines

A local, solo mobile web prototype designed for portrait screens. Open `index.html` in a modern browser, or serve this folder with `python -m http.server 4173` and visit `http://localhost:4173`.

Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard. You have 30 seconds to drag four fighters into place, or press **Ready**. During your turn, tap a fighter to select it, tap within its green movement circle to move, swipe to aim the team's shots, then press **Shoot**. The computer takes the opposing turns. The match ends when one team is eliminated or 40 turns are played.

The four fighters have different health, damage, spread, area damage, and cooldowns. Bullets can hit friendly fighters.
